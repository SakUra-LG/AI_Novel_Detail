# Codex Retranslation Specification

## Source of truth

The Chinese files in `../texts` are the sole source of narrative content. The previous English files are not translation source material.

## Editorial boundary

- Preserve plot, actions, dialogue, emotional progression, and ending.
- Use natural, performable English rather than mirroring Chinese syntax.
- Do not expand the story or add cultural lectures.
- Remove only demonstrable generation residue: exact duplicated sentences or paragraphs, immediately repeated passages, and obvious stitching duplication.
- Do not silently repair substantive plot contradictions. Record them for source-side review instead.
