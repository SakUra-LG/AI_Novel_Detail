Cao Guojun held up the jade board, feeling very wronged: "I was talking about this board."

The patrollers saw Lü Dongbin's sword light flashing on the water's surface and became even more convinced that the newcomers were up to no good. Lü Dongbin was about to explain that it was just to part the waves, but Zhang Guolao happened to beat his fish drum at that moment. The shrimp soldiers heard the drum sound and eliminated their last bit of doubt: "With a sword and a drum, how can you say you're not here to pick a fight?"

Zhang Guolao stopped beating the drum: "This drum is usually not used for fighting."

"Today it sounds like it is."

It was too late. The sea churned up chaotic waves, which came at the eight immortals from all sides. Han Zhongli hurriedly used his fan to hold back the waves in front, but the wind blew Lan Caihe's flower basket towards Lü Dongbin. Lü Dongbin avoided the flower basket, and the water path that his sword had parted was now in front of the paper donkey. The paper donkey saw that the water's surface had suddenly opened up and refused to believe that this was a path.

Zhang Guolao beat the fish drum to urge it: "There's no water ahead, it's just right to walk."

The paper donkey retreated.

"It thinks there's no water, which is even more suspicious," He Xiangu said.

Li Tieguai came to assist on his gourd, but the gourd was blown past by Han Zhongli's wind. He stretched out his iron crutch to hook the flower basket, and Lan Caihe immediately hugged the basket handle: "This is a magical tool, not a dock."

"If I hook you correctly, it would be."

Han Xiangzi tried to use his flute sound to calm the waves, but the fish were scattered by the sudden surge of water, and the tune was covered by the sound of the sea. Cao Guojun used the jade board to calm one place, but another wave rose up beside it. He calmed the left side, and the right side was chaotic; he turned to the right side, and the lotus behind him was pushed away by the waves.

The eight immortals' abilities were all still present, but each of them was exerting force in a different direction. No one was sent back to the shore, but no one could continue forward either. They were forced to come together from their original scattered paths, and their magical tools almost got tangled up.

He Xiangu protected the lotus and reminded everyone not to rush to prove themselves right. Han Zhongli stopped fanning, and Li Tieguai no longer stretched out his crutch to grab other people's magical tools. The commotion on the sea gradually subsided, and the patrollers could finally hear complete sentences.

Lü Dongbin sheathed his treasure sword: "Let's not fight for the road. The more we move, the more it looks like we're here to pick a fight."

Han Xiangzi also stopped playing his flute. The sea finally became a bit quieter. Lü Dongbin loudly explained to the patrollers that they were just passing by, had no intention of disturbing the Dragon Palace, and didn't want to hurt anyone.

The other side didn't immediately believe them. The shrimp soldiers asked, "If you're just passing by, why does everyone have their magical tools?"

Lan Caihe replied, "It's because we didn't bring a boat."

"You didn't bring a boat, so you brought a sword?"

Lü Dongbin turned the sword blade downwards: "This matter doesn't sound very convincing, does it?"

He Xiangu told everyone to stabilize their magical tools and stop competing to see who could go faster. Li Tieguai pressed down on the gourd, Han Zhongli reduced the wind from his fan, Zhang Guolao told the paper donkey to stop, and Lan Caihe also pulled all the flower branches back into the basket. The wind and waves were still ahead, and the misunderstanding about the Dragon Palace hadn't been completely cleared up.

For the first time, the Eight Immortals clearly saw that if they each showed off their own abilities, they could leave the shore; but if the eight of them only thought of themselves, they would not be able to get far in the same turbulent waves.

The turbulent waves didn't give them time to discuss things slowly. As soon as He Xiangu's lotus flower stabilized, a horizontal wave came from the side. Cao Guojiu used his jade plate to press down the water nearby, managing to keep her footing.

He Xiangu nodded at him without any formalities. At this point, saying one more sentence wouldn't make the waves come any less frequently just because of politeness.

Han Xiangzi raised his jade flute again. This time, he didn't lure fish or calm the waves alone; he just blew out a clear, short tune. The melody moved forward when it started and stopped when it ended, and all eight of them could hear it.

Han Zhongli gently swayed his banana leaf fan according to the tune, sending the wind towards the others behind him. Tieguai Li stood on the slowest side, using his iron crutch to point at the water, preventing the gourd from spinning again. Zhang Guolao was still riding his paper donkey backwards, able to see the waves behind; whenever he hit the fish drum, the others would know that waves were coming.

Lü Dongbin no longer used his sword to cut open a long path, only cutting through the most urgent wave in front of him. As soon as a path appeared, Cao Guojiu immediately used his jade plate to stabilize the entrance. He Xiangu's lotus flower went through first, followed closely by Lan Caihe's flower basket. If someone was pushed off course by a side wave, the lotus leaf would support one side, and the flower basket would block the other, not letting anyone fall behind alone.

A sudden backflow came from behind, heading straight for the gourd at the end. Zhang Guolao, facing the direction they came from, saw it first, and the fish drum sounded urgently. Han Zhongli immediately turned his fan to send a side wind, and Tieguai Li used his iron crutch to point at the water, taking advantage of the momentum to bring the gourd back to the group. If they were still going their separate ways like before, this time it would have been pushed far away alone.

Lan Caihe said, "You told me not to hook onto the lotus flower earlier, but now it's come to pick me up."

He Xiangu replied, "It can pick you up. Just take care of your flowers."

"They've made a lot of progress today and now only hook onto our own people."

"That doesn't exactly sound reassuring."

The group's laughter only lasted for a moment, but their movements below didn't become disordered. The sea wind picked up a gentle hum, the flute sound started again, and the Eight Immortals' figures moved like floating clouds and flowing water on the wave crests, walking shoulder to shoulder.

First, Tieguai Li swung his crutch, and the tip shot out a golden light, transforming into a thin silver rope that stably tied itself to a reef on the shore, as if leaving a path for himself. However, as soon as the rope tightened, the sea waves suddenly surged, lifting the rope with the waves, and Tieguai Li stumbled, his crutch falling into the water with a "thud," and Cao Guojiu laughed, saying, "Brother Li, it seems this rope is also afraid of water!"

Han Zhongli wasn't flustered; with a light touch of his feather duster, the dust turned into a gentle, soft breeze that pushed away the oncoming waves. The wind force weakened slightly but blew the nearby seaweed into a wild dance, accidentally brushing against Lü Dongbin's ankles. Lü Dongbin was startled, his foot slipped, and he almost fell into the water; he quickly used his sword to draw an arc-shaped light strip, stabilizing his figure, and the sword tip gently touched the sea surface, creating small ripples, and laughter followed: "The sword path doesn't cut off the paper donkey, but it did 'cut off' my foot!"

Zhang Guolao shook his donkey, which had a bronze plate on its back reflecting starlight. Zhang Guolao chanted a spell, and the bronze plate spun, transforming into a huge lotus leaf that floated on the water's surface, capable of carrying him and the donkey forward. However, as soon as the lotus leaf unfolded, the sea waves came again, and the lotus leaf was tossed about, on the verge of capsizing. Zhang Guolao shouted, "This lotus leaf also loves to 'swing dance'!"

He Xiangu gently stroked the jade plate in her hand, and it emitted a soft glow, transforming into a light strip that paved the water's surface like a silver road. As she stepped on it, the light strip trembled slightly, accidentally rolling up Lan Caihe's blue robe, and Lan Caihe exclaimed, "My robe is going to float away with me!" Lan Caihe quickly used his bamboo flute to blow a clear, crisp tune, and the flute's notes turned into small water bubbles that stuck to the robe, stabilizing it instantly. Lan Caihe laughed, saying, "This robe can 'soak' in the water."

The flute in Han Xiangzi's hand was suddenly blown askew by the sea wind, with a few water droplets coming out of the flute mouth, almost disrupting the tune. He quickly put the flute away and hummed a light, cheerful melody, which turned into a gentle breeze that helped Han Xiangzi step over a protruding reef smoothly.

Cao Guojiu shook his golden bell, and the sound was as clear as pearls falling onto a jade plate. The golden bell's light cast golden light columns on the water's surface, guiding the way forward. However, the light columns were too bright, startling a few small fish into scattering, and Cao Guojiu laughed, saying, "It seems my golden bell also wants to turn on the lights for the fish."

The eight immortals each showed off their magical abilities, and although their treasures had unique effects, they collided and joked with each other in the face of the turbulent East Sea. Each time their treasures lost control, it made them laugh awkwardly, but it also made their movements more coordinated.

When the last wave was dissolved by Han Xiangzi's gentle breeze, the light strips, lotus leaves, sword lights, crutch ropes, golden bell light columns, and flute sounds intertwined to form a rainbow-like bridge. The Eight Immortals stepped on it together, steadily moving towards the other shore.

The sea creatures watched from behind the waves for a long time. If the Eight Immortals really wanted to attack the Dragon Palace, they could have taken the sword's open path straight down to the depths; but all their actions were directed towards the other shore, and they made sure not to harm any of the sea creatures. The shrimp soldiers finally understood and waved their flags to have their companions withdraw the waves.

Once the waves were withdrawn, the natural sea breeze started blowing in from the front again. The Eight Immortals didn't scatter and rush forward; instead, they continued to follow the sound of the flute. It wasn't until each of their magical tools had passed the last rapid currents that Han Xiangzi blew a long note, signaling to everyone that they could head towards the shore.

The sea surface ahead gradually opened up. Han Xiangzi's melody didn't stop, and the eight immortals continued to move in the same rhythm, completing the final leg of their journey. Iron Crutch Li's gourd reached the shallow water near the shore first, but he didn't rush to get ahead. He used his iron crutch to steady himself on one side and waited for the paper donkey, which was being pushed by the wind, to catch up.

Zhang Guo Lao asked, "What are you waiting for? I can still see the way we came."

"That's exactly why someone needs to tell you that we've reached the shore," Iron Crutch Li said.

The paper donkey stepped onto the wet sand, and only then did Zhang Guo Lao turn around. The others also arrived on shore one by one: Han Zhongli closed his fan, Lü Dongbin sheathed his sword, He Xiangu held her lotus flower, Lan Caihe picked up his flower basket, Han Xiangzi put down his jade flute, and Cao Guojiu put away his jade tablet.

There were eight people, not one missing.

They had successfully crossed the sea, but none of them could say that they had relied solely on themselves for the final leg of the journey. Their unique abilities had allowed them to enter the East Sea, and it was their mutual support that had enabled them to exit the sea together.

Once they reached the other shore, the Eight Immortals put away their magical tools. Iron Crutch Li checked if his gourd had taken in any water, Lan Caihe busied himself putting the flower branches back into his basket, and Zhang Guo Lao patted the paper donkey's neck, praising it for knowing the direction all along.

Lü Dongbin asked, "If it knew the direction, why did it still need someone to remind it that we had reached the shore?"

Zhang Guo Lao said, "That's because the shore arrived too suddenly."

He Xiangu pointed behind them, "The shore has been here all along."

"That's why I said it was patient," Zhang Guo Lao added.

Cao Guojiu wanted to thank everyone, but Han Zhongli stopped him, "Don't thank us yet. If you start with Iron Crutch Li and thank each of us in turn, the sun will be setting by the time you're done."

Iron Crutch Li asked, "Why start with me?"

"Because your gourd turned the most, taking care of every direction," Han Zhongli replied.

"That was because of the fan," Iron Crutch Li said.

Han Zhongli immediately looked away, "The sea wind was very strong; we can't attribute everything to the fan."

Han Xiangzi laughed and blew a short note, as if putting an end to the argument. The group didn't try to determine who was the most important, and they no longer pretended that they had each traveled the final leg alone. The lotus flower had supported the deviating flower basket, the jade tablet had stabilized the waves in front of the lotus flower, the sword had opened the path, the banana fan had sent the wind, the fish drum had warned of the following waves, the gourd had guarded the slow areas, and the jade flute had connected all the actions together. If any one of these had been missing, they wouldn't have been able to stand on the shore together as they were now.

After the wind and waves had subsided, the old fisherman brought Aman to the other shore along the coastline. When Aman got off the boat, he held the green bamboo slips tightly and paid the boat fare. Lan Caihe saw him and asked, "We each used our unique abilities, but you took a boat. Why?"

Aman replied, "I'm in charge of recording the abilities, not pretending to have one myself."

The old fisherman nodded in agreement, "This young man's talent is that he didn't cause any trouble on the way."

Aman wrote this down as well, but he felt that it didn't quite fit with the rest of the text, so he drew a small circle next to it, planning to pass it off as an ink blot later.

He asked the Eight Immortals who had helped the most during the final leg of the journey. The eight of them pointed to each other in a circle, and surprisingly, not a single finger pointed to themselves. Aman counted twice but eventually gave up ranking them. Instead, he wrote on the green bamboo slips: "The Eight Immortals crossed the sea, each using their unique abilities; when the wind and waves arose, each had their own strengths, and together they accomplished the task, with no one left behind."

After finishing his writing, Aman didn't ask the Eight Immortals to reenact their journey or add himself as the ninth member. As he closed the green bamboo slips, Iron Crutch Li had already slung his gourd over his shoulder, Han Zhongli was shaking his banana fan, and Zhang Guo Lao was still riding his paper donkey; Lü Dongbin, He Xiangu, Lan Caihe, Han Xiangzi, and Cao Guojiu were all packed up and ready to go.

Lan Caihe turned to ask the old fisherman, "Can your boat really hold eight people?"

The old fisherman replied, "Not anymore."

"Why not?" Lan Caihe asked.

"After listening to your stories, my boat feels like it's not capable enough," the old fisherman said.

Everyone laughed. The Eight Immortals didn't cross the sea again to prove anything; instead, they continued along the mountain road on the other shore. The East Sea was still creating waves behind them, and from then on, the phrase "The Eight Immortals crossed the sea, each using their unique abilities" not only referred to the eight magical tools but also remembered the final leg of their journey, where no one traveled alone.
